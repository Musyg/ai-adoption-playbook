# Code of conduct

This repository welcomes evidence-based corrections, practical improvements,
and respectful disagreement.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss claims, methods, and evidence rather than attacking people.
- State uncertainty and distinguish observation, inference, and opinion.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual property.
- Give contributors specific, actionable feedback.
- Correct mistakes openly when better evidence becomes available.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misrepresentation, publication
of private information, and attempts to submit secrets or confidential client
material are not accepted.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove content
that violates it and may restrict participation when necessary.

Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue when doing so would expose a
person or sensitive material. Contact the maintainer privately using the contact
method published on the repository owner profile. Platform abuse can also be
reported through GitHub's reporting tools.
